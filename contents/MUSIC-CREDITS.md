# Live-radio sources — WebThen v0.14

- **181.FM — Star 90’s:** https://player.181fm.com/?station=181-star90s
- **1.FM — y2k / 2000s Hits:** https://www.1.fm/station/hits2000
- **1.FM — 90s Best:** https://www.1.fm/station/90s

181.FM publishes its direct streams at https://www.181.fm/links and explains using stream links for your own music player setup at https://www.181.fm/support. Its embed customization page is https://www.181.fm/codes. 1.FM publishes the stream URLs in the public data of its station pages.

Audio remains on the broadcasters' servers, with their programming and ads intact. No audio files, recording, or rebroadcast service are included. These source links do not grant WebThen ownership of songs or independent music-distribution rights. radio-evidence.json records the exact public stream URLs and checks.
